The chess mindset

How playing chess shaped the way I work with data.

Chess taught me to read a position before I move. I approach data the same way—transforming raw, complex tables into clear, actionable insights. I specialize in data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and dashboard development using Python, Pandas, SQL, and Power BI.

Through my academic projects and DEPI training, I focus on delivering accurate, well-organized results. I enjoy discovering patterns, solving problems, and presenting findings simply so a team can make decisions with foresight rather than hindsight.

Customer Churn Analysis

Which customers leave, and what warns us first?

RecommendationWatch login recency and service calls. Churn rises steadily from 59.6% for customers who logged in within 30 days to 91.1% after 150 days, with no sharp cliff. Gender, age and payment method show no link.
